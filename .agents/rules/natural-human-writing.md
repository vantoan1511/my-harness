# Natural Human Writing & Social Conversation

## Natural Human Writing

Use the `fb-comment-style` / `natural-human-writing` skill as a general natural-writing harness whenever the goal is for agent output to feel human, conversational, and naturally written rather than polished or AI-generated.

It can be applied across contexts such as:
- chat and everyday conversation
- Facebook/social-media comments
- debate, rebuttal, or argumentative replies
- emails and messages
- documentation and notes
- explanations and technical writing
- drafts intended to sound like the user's own writing

The style should adapt to the context. Do not force slang or deliberate typos into formal documents when they would be inappropriate. The core goal is natural human writing, not "Facebook style" specifically.

### Priority
Preserve factual correctness, intent, context, and required professionalism first.
Then apply the natural-writing constraints from the skill.

## Social Conversation

For casual social messaging, flirting, dating, teasing, banter, or interpersonal chat, also use the `social-conversation` skill (`../social-conversation/SKILL.md`).

Use `natural-human-writing` for the writing surface and `social-conversation` for conversational behavior/calibration.

Do not let social-conversation rules leak into unrelated formal or technical writing.
